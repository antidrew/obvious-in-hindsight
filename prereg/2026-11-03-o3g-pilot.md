# O3g pilot: can Gemma write the tests that Claude's O3b tests were? (prereg 2026-11-03, before any O3g run)

**Instrument. It adopts nothing.** No result here promotes an agent, and agent_o3g is not a submission candidate. O3b's Claude-written tests and all oracle data stay in `research/`, never ship and never train. agent_o3g does not read or mention them; `scripts/preflight.sh` enforces this on the agent directory. O3g's own tests are scored, not reused, unless a later prereg says so.

## Observation
`reports/rethink-2026-11-02/o3-contract.md` section 4 and `o3b-gate.md`, on the 14 strict flips of O3 (control 0/2, O3 passed).
- **O3b gate:** Claude's tests, written from issue and base repo without gold, kill control on **5 of 9** non-D tasks (fastmcp, moto, gitpython, msal, cfn), 0 of 5 D, gold pass 157/205 = 76.6%. Falsifier was at most 2.
- **That 5 is fragile:** without cfn 4 (r1 adds only a YAML, so any valid test kills); without the kills resting on recalled content (moto, gitpython) 3; without every Recalled-docstring task (also msal, loguru) 2, on the falsifier; also without cfn, 2 and 1. Flips were selected on O3's outcome, one rater, at most two controls per task. Counting tests that pass on base and fail on control: 7 strict, 8 any.
- **Against Gemma:** on the 8 A/B/C flips, the control's last own check was green on a wrong patch in 10 of 16 attempts: its checks share its misconception (o3-contract section 2). E10 made test-first happen with pooled delta 0.
- **Control's time:** mean 244 and 234 s per attempt on F51 (`results/ood-oracle20261103-r{1,2}-agent_e18`, `duration_seconds`), 207 and 209 s on these 14.

## Hypothesis
The contract O3 supplied can come from a strong spec writer but not from Gemma: asked only to write tests, in 10 calls, it writes the same cases its solving attempts check, so its tests pass on the wrong patches.

## Assumptions
1. **The file reaches the patch.** The harness strips `test_*.py` and `*_test.py` (`swegemma/harness/verification.py:62`, applied in `execution.py` submit_patch and `agent_runner.py:1069,1101`), so the planned `/workspace/test_o3g.py` would be lost. The agent writes `o3g_spec.py` at the root instead. Checked: real `git diff` through the harness's strip drops `test_o3g.py`, keeps `o3g_spec.py`; no root `.gitignore` of the 14 ignores it.
2. **The test's file name does not matter.** The rig (`o3b-gate/run_variant.py`, unchanged, hash-pinned) runs a file named `test_o3b.py`, so each extracted file is saved under that name.
3. **A killed patch is a contract catch.** Same limit as O3b: cfn is killed by anything valid, and a test can fail on control for another reason. Read the kill tests before believing them.
4. **The prompt carries no pilot answers.** Its edge-case list is generic test design (supplied values and defaults, empty and boundary inputs, input forms, named entry points). It names no task, domain or gold case, and was written with o3-contract's examples in view but not quoted. No reference to scoring.
5. **Flips are upper-biased** for any test writer. This favours the falsifier side, so a low count is the stronger reading.
6. **Prompt form, not ability.** One prompt and two passes cannot separate "Gemma cannot" from "this prompt did not teach it". A low reading is "not in 10 calls with this prompt". SD at these counts is about 1 per pass (judgement), hence the mean of two.

## Score
`scripts/score_o3g.py`. O3b's rule, per pass: extract the file from the attempt's patch; run it twice on base, gold and control r1, r2 (`results/ood-oracle20261103-r{1,2}-agent_e18`; fastmcp and cfn have one). A test is scored if it passes on gold and fails on base. A task kills if every non-empty control patch fails at least one scored test. Prints O3b's sensitivities on the same task sets, and attempts with no usable tests (no file, no collection on gold, none pass gold, none fail base). The scorer is validated before any run: on O3b's saved runs (`--check`) and on O3b's frozen tests wrapped as patches through the whole path (`--standin`), it must reproduce `o3b-gate/table.tsv` and `sensitivity.json`.

## Prediction
Non-D kills **at most 2 of 9** (o3-contract section 4), mean of two passes. Beyond that, by judgement: cfn is the likeliest kill, D at most 1 of 5, gold pass under O3b's 76.6%, at least 2 of 14 attempts with no usable tests per pass. Any full run built on this: N32 at most 2.

## Decision rule
On the mean of passes of non-D kills, O3b's strict rule:
- **At most 2 of 9: Gemma cannot obtain the contract in 10 calls.** Close the Gemma-written section. The question becomes teaching it: a sub-skill LoRA trained on issue → test from public PRs (`training-data` rules hold: no dev40; own prereg).
- **At least 4 of 9 (falsifies):** read the traces and kill tests first, since this contradicts E10; then F51 × 2 with Gemma-written tests as a section, own prereg, with a time budget against 12 h below.
- **3, or anything strictly between 2 and 4 (2.5, 3.5):** one more pass, r3, about 30 min, then the rule on the mean of three.

Operational rules, before any run:
1. The verdict is the registered 9 including cfn, as O3b's was. Sensitivities are not the verdict.
2. A pass with rig failures (printed, exit 2) is re-run before reading.
3. **Delivery guard.** If at most 2 comes with more than a third of non-D attempts delivering nothing that runs (no file, or no collection on gold), it is a delivery problem and not yet a contract reading: traces first, branch held, one prompt or budget fix allowed by its own prereg.
4. If at least 4 holds only with cfn, or rests only on flaky tests, say so with the verdict. It stands, and F51 is not exempt from reading kill tests.
5. D kills, gold pass and no-usable counts are read with the verdict and change no branch.

**Time budget for the 4-of-9 branch.** The limit is 277 s of mean agent time per hidden task: 12 h over 120 tasks at slowdown 1.3 (`kb/harness.md`: Kaggle at most about 1.3× our concurrency-3 runs; hidden count and sequential scoring as the briefing assumes, unverified). Control already spends 234-244 s on F51, leaving 33-43 s. This pilot's mean attempt time is the pre-pass's cost, so a 2-minute pre-pass means taking about 90 s from the solver, whose budget is itself then a tested change. Any Kaggle candidate needs its own prediction; an in-agent pre-pass also differs in form from the section replayed by F51.

## Mechanism counters
- Delivery: attempts with the file in the patch, tool calls, seconds, writes to other paths (from traces), source edits in the patch.
- Validity: gold pass, tests scored, vacuous tests, flaky tests, hung runs.
- Contract: for each kill, the test that kills, and whether it is the case O3b's table names (fastmcp new parser, moto supplied period, gitpython URL match, msal host with scheme). Read every one.
- Comparison with own checks: does a Gemma test exercise a case the control's own check did not?

## Amendment 2: delivery fix (written 2026-11-03, before any agent_o3g2 run)
Under operational rule 3. This file has no Amendment 1; the name is as requested. Basis: `reports/rethink-2026-11-02/o3g-traces.md`.

**Observation.** O3g mean 0.5 of 9, guard fired at 12 of 18. Of the 12: 8 budget (first write refused after call 10), 1 missing-`filepath` loop, 1 path `o3g_spec.py%`, 2 backslash-escaped quotes. Files carrying the rejected writes, run post hoc through the same rig, give a mean of 1.0 of 9, cfn only. Post hoc, not a verdict.

**Hypothesis.** Delivery failed on budget and on `write_file`'s argument form. With both removed, the reading becomes a contract reading, and stays low because the decisive case is usually absent from Gemma's own reasoning (11 of 18 attempts).

**Assumptions.**
1. A heredoc is near-immune. Pool: 482 Python heredocs in 429 agent_e18 attempts, no call lost its `command`, 20 (4.1%) hold a backslash-quote; 36 fail a raw parse, an upper bound since 481 of 491 were unquoted (shell rewrites them). `write_file` here: an escaped-quote file in 11 of 28 attempts. The pools differ (scratch scripts vs deliverable), and the model can still escape quotes in a heredoc.
2. Gemma does not count calls: despite "write by call 7", the first write attempt came after call 7 in 18 of 28 attempts and after call 10 in 12. 20 calls leave margin even if it keeps ignoring the limit.
3. More calls may also raise contract content, since it reads more. This arm cannot separate that, and it is not meant to.

**Arm.** `agent_o3g2`, three parts of one delivery fix. Rule 3 says "one prompt or budget fix"; this bundles them on purpose, because the faults compete, and it adopts nothing.
| Change | Value | Origin |
|---|---|---|
| Budget | `max_tool_calls` 20, `max_time_minutes` 10, `max_turns` 60 | Pilot's ratios doubled; 18 s per call means 20 calls is about 360 s |
| Tools | `write_file` removed; file written by one quoted `run_command` heredoc, repair by writing it whole again | judge1 section A; these traces |
| Prompt | Read at most 8, write by call 10, then one run, one repair and rerun, stop and submit; no backslash before quotes; no docstrings; what refuses after the limit | These traces |
Section 1 of the prompt (behaviours and edge cases) and the sampling file are byte-identical. The prompt does not mention scoring: the "counts" wording in Budget was replaced. Tag `o3g2a20261103`, results `results/ood-o3g2a20261103-r{1,2}-agent_o3g2`, job `queue/held/56-o3g2.sh`, tasks and DATA as before.

**Score.** Same: `scripts/score_o3g.py --tag o3g2a20261103 --agent agent_o3g2`, O3b's rule, same rig (hash pinned), same control patches. The scorer gains `--agent` and counts heredoc writes in the mechanism counter; `--check` still reproduces O3b, and `--report-only` on O3g's saved runs reprints the log with only the `writes` column changed on two lines.

**Decision rule.** Unchanged, mean of two passes, at 20 calls instead of 10: at most 2 of 9 closes the Gemma-written section and moves to teaching; at least 4 reads traces and kill tests first, then F51 × 2 by own prereg; strictly between, r3. Rules 1, 4 and 5 unchanged.

**Delivery guard.** Same: more than a third of non-D attempts (over 6 of 18) with no file or no collection on gold means delivery again, not a contract reading. No further fix without a new prereg.

**Rule 2 reading.** `RIG FAILURES` on a task with no test file is a known label artefact (exit 2 included). A rig failure is real only if an attempt with a file is listed, which is also `rig_fail` above 0 in the no-usable count. Those are re-run before reading.

**Time.** If the 4-of-9 branch opens, about 360 s exceeds the 277 s per-task limit on its own. This arm measures contract content; it is not a shippable pre-pass.

**Prediction.** Delivery: at most 4 of 18 non-D attempts deliver nothing that runs (guard does not fire); test file in the patch on at least 12 of 14 per pass; missing-`filepath` faults 0. Kills: mean **at most 2 of 9**, by judgement 1 to 2, cfn plus at most one of fastmcp and gitpython. Gold pass at or under O3b's 76.6%. Moto, loguru and msal not killed on both passes.

**Falsifiers.** Guard fires again: the fix failed, and the contract question stays open. Mean at least 4: the delivery failure was hiding real contract knowledge, against these traces.
