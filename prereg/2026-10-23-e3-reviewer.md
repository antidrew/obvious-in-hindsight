# Pre-registration E3: a fresh-context reviewer of the diff (agent_e3 vs agent_e2b)

Written 2026-10-23 07:10 UTC, before any E3 run.

## Why
pub22/e2b failures on OOD-45 that are not budget-bound are wrong patches submitted with budget left: missed
sibling sites, message case, collateral edits (E2 analysis). The author's own claim check (E2b) did not reduce
them (wrong 10/90 vs 9/90): the author verifies what it believes it wrote. A reviewer with fresh context sees
only the issue and the diff. Strategies: iterative prompting (a critique drives one more pass), easier
precursor problem ("find the defect in this diff" instead of "solve the bug"), decomposition (four review
questions). Uses the unused time budget (pub22 ~3 of ~6 min per task) in a structured way.

## Change (one design, its cost included)
`agent_e3` = `agent_e2b` + `reviewer` sub-agent (read-only: run_command, read_file; thinking 2048; <= 3 calls;
one-line `ACCEPT` / `DEFECT: file:line - what - change`), called once after the check script (prompt step 4b),
skipped when < 6 calls or 90 s remain. Cap 28 -> 32 to pay for the review (~4 calls). E2a showed that raw
budget (44) adds churn; +4 here is bound to a single review step.

## Runs
Interleaved, 2 passes each, one task per process, 3 processes: OOD-45 (tag `20261023e3`, primary), then RB64
(tag `20261023e3rb`, secondary hard set). ~8 h of GPU.

## Predictions
- OOD-45: e3 - e2b = +1.0 per pass (plausible 0 to +2.5); wrong patches <= 0.7 x e2b's.
- RB64: +1.5 (plausible -1 to +3); at-cap rate rises by <= 5 points.
- Mechanism: reviewer called in >= 70% of attempts; DEFECT in 20-50% of calls; >= half of DEFECTs followed by an
  edit. If the reviewer is called < 50%, the step is not happening and the outcome says nothing about review.
- Not worse than e2b by more than 1 task per pass on OOD-45.

## Decision
Keep e3 if OOD-45 gain >= +0.5 and RB not worse than -1, with the mechanism counters met; it becomes the next
Kaggle candidate. If the outcome is up but the reviewer was rarely called, attribute it to the +4 calls, not review.

## Outcome (2026-10-23 18:00 UTC, 2 passes per arm)
- **OOD-45: e3 - e2b = -1.50/pass** (39.0 vs 40.5; p for a gain 0.92); wrong 10 vs 8/90. **RB64: -2.00**
  (24.0 vs 26.0; p 0.90); empty 18 vs 18, wrong 62 vs 58/128. Predictions (+1.0 / +1.5) **failed**; the
  "not worse than -1 on OOD" bound failed.
- Mechanism: reviewer called in 73/90 OOD attempts (81%, bound 70% met) but only 67/128 on RB (52%; 44 of the
  61 RB attempts without a review failed: the budget ran out first). Explicit `DEFECT` lines in ~8 of 73 OOD
  reviews (below the 20-50% band); some were harmful (feast_6874: asked to revert the correct sibling fix).
- **Design flaw found:** ADK `agent_tool` does not give the sub-agent fresh context. Its request was the root's
  own summary ("For context: [model] said: ..."), so the reviewer inherited the author's framing. The verbatim
  issue is available as session state `problem_description` (`agent_runner.py:676`), usable as
  `{problem_description}` in a sub-agent instruction.
- **Decision: rejected.** e2b stays the base. Two added-part designs in a row failed (E2a, E3), so by the
  ablate-toward-simpler rule the next run removes or simplifies instead of adding.
