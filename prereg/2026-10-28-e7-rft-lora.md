# E7: rejection-sampling fine-tuning (RFT) of a LoRA on a large, diverse SWE-rebench training pool

Written 2026-10-28, before any data exists. Goal: a real mean gain (top-10 needs +5-6/58; prompt tuning is at a
plateau of six null single changes).

## Why this is not v19 again
v19 (no effect) trained on 745 examples from 3 repos (synthetic bugs + 12 mined fastapi PRs) of an older agent,
and the adapter learned style (held-out loss fell as much as training loss). E7 differs in the three ways that
v19's diagnosis points at:
1. **Diversity:** ~400-500 real issues from ~400 repos (full SWE-rebench, CC-BY-4.0; well-specified, 2022+,
   disjoint from public / OOD-45 / RB repos; `REBENCH_OUT=data/rebench_full scripts/build_rebench.py`).
2. **Selection on the frontier:** train on successful runs of tasks the agent solves only sometimes (1-3 of 4
   samples), where a success carries information about what distinguishes it from a failure. Always-solved
   tasks are down-weighted (style), never-solved give nothing.
3. **On-policy, current agent:** agent_e2b's own trajectories (same prompts and tools as at test time).

## Data
`scripts/collect_pool.sh` (GPU-queue filler): agent_e2b, 4 passes over `dev_sets/train_rb.txt` (gold passes,
empty fails), every model call logged per task (`train/raw_rb/p<N>/<task>.jsonl`).

## Hypothesis and predictions
H: on-policy successes from frontier tasks on many repos teach behaviour that transfers to unseen repos.
1. Mechanism check before any eval: held-out loss on *failed* runs falls clearly less than on held-out
   *successful* runs (v19: both fell equally). If they fall equally, stop: the adapter learns style again.
2. Outcome: e7 − e2b ≥ +1.5/pass pooled over OOD-45 + rb64 (2 passes each), p ≤ 0.15, OOD ≥ −1.
3. Cost: mean time per task ≤ 1.15 × e2b (LoRA costs 0-13% in graph mode).
Reject if 1 fails, or pooled ≤ 0.

## Risks noted in advance
Others report SFT ≤ prompt-only (forum 751596, incl. 2,000 teacher runs). Trajectories with thinking may
exceed the trainer's ~10k-token limit at 32 GB; measure on the first collected pass and cut at the first
repeated/truncated turn as in `train/build_sft.py`.

## Addendum 2026-10-29 (before any training; data facts only)
Data after 3 passes (178 usable tasks): solved 0/3 79, 1/3 14, 2/3 8, 3/3 77; mixed tasks are scarce (22).
Builder: `train/build_rft.py` (thinking kept: labels the model's own history turns incl. thoughts; one example per
conversation because events compaction splits long runs; max 10,240 tokens). Dry run on passes 1-3: 162 training
examples (51 from mixed tasks, 111 from always-solved), 390k trained tokens; held out 20% of tasks (stratified).
Training: rank 16, alpha 32, lr 1e-4, 2 epochs (`train/train_lora.py`), on passes 1-4.
**Mechanism rule, numeric:** per-token NLL on held-out examples, base vs adapter (`train/score_heldout.py`).
Let d_ok and d_fail be the relative NLL drops on held-out successful and failed runs. Pass if d_fail < 0.75 × d_ok
(the adapter prefers success-like behaviour, not just the agent's style). v19 had d_fail ≈ d_ok.
If the rule passes, the queue runs the outcome eval automatically: agent_e7 (= e2b + adapter) vs e2b, OOD-45 × 2 and
rb64 × 2, interleaved, same LoRA-enabled server for both arms. If it fails, no eval; E7 rejected as "style only".
