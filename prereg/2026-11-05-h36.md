# H36: the ladder on hidden-like tasks (2026-11-05, before any H36 run)

**Instrument. Adopts nothing; gold-derived data never ships or trains** (same firewall as `2026-11-03-oracle-ladder.md`).

## Observation
- The ladder (`agent_e18`, F51): tests move solves far more than exact location (N32 control 1.0, O2 2.0, O3 12.5). F51 was selected on rb64 outcomes and rb64 scores about 36%, Kaggle about 15%.
- So the wall we measured may not be the hidden wall. This is the largest threat to the paper's generality (`kb/evidence-2026-11-05.md` section 5). Never tested.
- `judge-final.md` section 2: natural SWE-rebench tasks with no code in the statement and at least 3 gold hunks solve at 0.178 (n 36, e2b); the same cell on public dev33k at 0.13 (n 10). Public 129 has 33% of its tasks in this cell.

## Question
On tasks selected by features and not by outcomes, at a level close to Kaggle's, does the same information buy the same solves? Or does location start to matter once the statement has no anchors and the fix has several sites?

## Set
`dev_sets/rb_h36.txt`, built by `scripts/h36_tasks.py` with the same pool and feature code as `judge/k_test.py`: 36 tasks, 32 repos, mean e2b rate 0.178 (reproduced). 7 are also in F51 (5 N32, 2 M19). **H29, the 29 outside F51, have never been in the ladder**, and are the check against reuse of F51. All 29 are in `dev_sets/train_rb.txt` (gold validated, used to collect e2b traces); `agent_e18` carries no trained weights, so that use cannot leak into this run.

## Assumptions
1. No code and at least 3 hunks is one plausible description of hard hidden tasks, not a known one. The hosts' filter is not applied. A result here is about this cell.
2. Agents' e2b rates on these tasks predict e18's level (rb64: 24.6 and 25.0 per pass).
3. Two passes give SE about 1.5 per arm at a true 6-8, so about 2.1 on a difference: +4 is about 2 SE. Smaller effects are not resolved.
4. **Long sections may break delivery.** `leo_editor_3678` has a 59k-character test patch (60-63k section, F51's largest about 10k). e18 pins the statement in the system prompt, and the context is 32k tokens. It runs as is; every reading is repeated with `--exclude leo_editor_3678`, and if that changes a rule's outcome the exclusion is reported, not silently chosen.
5. O3 cannot be run by this agent, only read (as on F51).

## Arms (agent_e18, byte-identical; only the statement's section differs; hash check at job start)

## Predictions (mean solved per pass, of 36)
- Control 4-9.
- O2 6-11 (F51 showed little, but I expect location to matter somewhat more with no anchors).
- O3 12-20.
- O23 14-24.
- O6 at least 28.

## Reading rules (fixed now; `scripts/read_h36.py`)
Primary: H36, one-sided within-task sign-flip against this job's control. Two tests (O3, O2), not corrected; the +4 threshold applies too. H29 reported beside it: a rule that holds on H36 but not in direction on H29 is reported as resting on F51's tasks.
1. **Same wall:** O3 ≥ +4 with p ≤ 0.05, and O2 ≤ +2. The paper can say the ordering held on a feature-selected, Kaggle-level set.
2. **Location matters here:** O2 ≥ +4 with p ≤ 0.05. The F51 reading does not generalise, and the paper must say so; the sites-finder and graph work is reopened on this set.
3. **Needs both:** neither alone moves, and O23 (one pass) at least +6. Then one more O23 pass before reading.
4. **Information does not buy it:** O3, O2 and O23 all at most +2 with O6 at least 28. The wall is reasoning about multi-site fixes, and the ladder stays a claim about F51 only.
5. **Delivery wall:** O6 below 24. Read traces before anything above; applying known multi-site diffs is itself failing.
6. **Calibration:** control mean at least 12 means this set is not hard for e18, and it does not calibrate to Kaggle's level.
Anything else is unresolved and reported as numbers.

Whichever way it falls, it goes in the paper. **It cannot explain the rb-Kaggle gap, and does not show hidden tasks look like this.**

## Mechanism checks
`read_ladder` counters per arm: section at first call, `lost` (over 30% means a low reading of that arm is unreadable), empties, wrong, calls, minutes, hunks touched. O2 and O23 must raise hunks touched, or the agent ignored them. Five O6 failures are read if O6 < 28.

## Limits
Thirty-six tasks, one agent, one feature definition chosen post hoc in `judge-final.md`. Gold information cannot be supplied on hidden tasks, so no result here is a route to score.
