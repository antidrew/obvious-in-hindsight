# E25: distil 31B's solved runs into the E4B model (2026-11-07, before any run)

**Score lever for the small-model prize; also a paper data point (scale and training).** Training data is agent runs on repo-disjoint public tasks only; no oracle or research data (`train/oracle_guard.py`), no evaluation task and no evaluation repo.

## Observation (E4B round 1, `reports/prereg/2026-11-07-e4b-round1.md`)
- E4B submits on its own after a median of 8-10 tool calls (cap 28); 92 of 128 rb64 attempts with agent_e10 end in a wrong patch, 78 of them after running commands. Only 5-11 of 64 per pass end on the nudge limit.
- The 50-call arm (e10x) changed nothing on rb64 (22 vs 22 over two passes): budget is not binding. The limit is what E4B does with each call and when it decides it is done, not how many calls it may make.
- E7 (31B trained on its own solved runs, 162 examples) was null on unseen tasks. Self-distillation has no teacher gap. Here the teacher solves about twice as often as the student (31B e10 about 36% of rb64; E4B about 17-22%), which is the usual setting where distillation of agent trajectories works in the literature (SWE-Gym, SWE-smith: hundreds to thousands of trajectories, larger students).

## Hypothesis and assumptions
H: E4B fine-tuned (LoRA) on 31B's successful agent_e10 runs solves more unseen tasks, because it copies the teacher's process (more reading before editing, checking before submitting), which E4B lacks.
1. 31B solves about 30-40% of the pool, so 480 tasks give about 140-190 solved runs. If fewer than 80 usable examples exist, training is not run.
2. The student can absorb protocol from a few hundred examples with rank 16; it cannot absorb the teacher's knowledge. So the gain, if any, comes with longer runs (more calls).
3. The 31B and E4B tokenisers are identical; the chat templates differ only when thinking is off (verified 11-07), so 31B-tokenised examples are exact for E4B with thinking on.
4. The hand-written LoRA trainer works on E4B's architecture (shared-KV layers, per-layer inputs). Unverified: the job runs a two-example smoke first and stops if it fails.

## Design
- **Pool:** `dev_sets/distill_pool.txt`: rebench_full tasks not in rb64, F51, H36 or OOD-45 and from none of their repos (980 tasks, 621 repos), at most 2 per repo, seed 0: 800 tasks. Part A = first 480, part B = the rest.
- **Job 89:** collect agent_e10 on 31B over part A (28 calls / 8 min, the student's exact prompt), logging every call (`SFT_DIR`). About 11 h.
- **Job 90:** build examples (`train/build_rft.py` with `RFT_RAW`/`RFT_RES`, max 16384 tokens, one success per solved task; 20% of tasks held out for the NLL check), train E4B LoRA (rank 16, alpha 32, lr 1e-4, 2 epochs; `train/train_lora.py --model-dir model_e4b`), serve E4B plus adapter `d1`, then interleave agent_e10 (base E4B) and agent_e25 (= agent_e10 + `adapter: d1`, nothing else changed) on rb64 x2 and OOD-45 x1. About 5 h.
- **Job 91:** collect part B (320 tasks, about 7.5 h) for a second, larger adapter whatever job 90 shows (learning-curve point).

## Predictions
- Yield: 140-190 solved runs of 480.
- Mechanism: held-out NLL of teacher successes falls (E4B base vs adapter) by at least 0.15 nats per trained token; student median tool calls on rb64 rises from 8-10 to 12-18.
- Outcome: agent_e25 − agent_e10 on rb64 +0 to +5 per pass; OOD-45 −1 to +3.

## Rules (fixed now)
1. **Adopt for the small-model track:** rb64 pooled difference ≥ +2 per pass, one-sided within-task sign-flip p ≤ 0.10, OOD not below −2, and predicted Kaggle time (local minutes × 1.75 × 120) ≤ 10 h. Then build the E4B zip (model field e4b, adapter under 3 GiB), preflight, and queue it for a Kaggle draw.
2. **Mechanism without outcome:** calls rise but solves do not: the student copies the form, not the judgement; report as a negative for protocol distillation at this data size, and use part B only as a learning-curve point.
3. **No mechanism:** NLL falls under 0.05 or calls unchanged: the adapter did not take; check training (loss curve, served adapter metrics) before reading anything.
4. **Harm:** rb64 ≤ −2 per pass: report; keep base E4B.

## Checks
Adapter actually served (vLLM `/v1/models` lists `d1`; LoRA request metrics). Tool-call parse errors per arm (a LoRA can break the format). Empty-patch rate. No evaluation task id or repo in the training set (asserted in the job).

## Limits
One teacher prompt, one adapter size, one seed. A gain shows that trajectory distillation helps this student on public-like tasks; it does not show transfer to the hidden set (section 2 of the evidence map: local-to-Kaggle transfer is untested).
